Hand bar

Each player gets one bar for everything they hold off the battlefield: ink, hand, discard, deck and lore. The ink becomes a ring with the ready count in the middle. The separate pile row is gone, so the battlefield runs from the bar to the centre line.

A · Side dockB · Hand barC · Scoreboard

Try it

  • Drag a card a few pixels left onto the ring to ink it. This is the shortest drag of the three proposals.
  • Click the ring for Spend 1, Ready 1, Ready all and the per-card controls.
  • Play a 4-cost card and watch four segments go dark.
  • Switch to Today to compare, and Show space to see the boxes.

Gains

  • Hand and ink sit side by side, which is where you look when you ask "can I play this?"
  • The ring reads as ready of total at any size, with no pips to count.
  • Lore, deck and discard stay in view right beside the hand.
  • On phones it replaces the hand strip, so no extra row is added.

Costs

  • The hand loses width: ring, piles and lore take about 240 px on desktop and 150 px on phone, so big hands overlap more.
  • The ring gets crowded past about 12 ink.
  • The opponent's ink sits at the top edge, far from yours.

Building it in app/index.html

  1. Fold .pile-row into .hand-wrap as a grid with the ring, hand, piles and lore. Remove the standalone row.
  2. Add a renderInkRing() beside renderInkCounter() that draws one SVG arc per ink card, ready first.
  3. Phone: the hand fan keeps its tap and drag-up behaviour, with the ring and deck pinned to either end of the strip.
  4. The panel opens above the ring for you and below it for the opponent, which is today's logic with a new anchor.
  5. Move .lore-badge into the bar.
