Side dock

Lore, ink, discard and deck stack into one slim column at the outer edge of each half. The full-width ink row goes away and the battlefield gets that height back. Both inkwells end up beside the centre line, level with each other, so you can compare them at a glance.

Try it

  • Drag a card from your hand onto the Ink well, or tap it and choose Ink it.
  • Click the well for Spend 1, Ready 1, Ready all and the per-card controls.
  • Next turn readies everything and turns last turn's ink face down.
  • Switch to Today to compare, and Show space to see the boxes.

Gains

  • The battlefield runs the full height of each half.
  • The well fills from the bottom, one hexagon per ink, and holds 16+ ink without a second row.
  • Lore joins the other numbers, so no badge floats over the cards.
  • Same shape on desktop and phone.

Costs

  • Takes about 100 px of width per half on desktop (60 px on phone). Fields rarely use their full width, so cards barely shrink.
  • The panel opens sideways and covers the outer edge of the field.
  • The count reads top-down, which is less familiar than a row.

Building it in app/index.html

  1. Make .board-half a two-column grid and replace both .pile-rows with a .dock in the second column. Move .lore-badge into it.
  2. renderInkCounter() keeps its pip markup. Only the container changes, to a two-column wrap-reverse grid so it fills upward.
  3. openInkPanel() anchors to the left of the dock instead of above or below the counter.
  4. The desktop overrides for .board-half.top .pile-row and .inkwell are no longer needed.
  5. Drop targets and every panel action stay as they are, so App.drop(event, 'inkwell', …) just moves onto the well.
