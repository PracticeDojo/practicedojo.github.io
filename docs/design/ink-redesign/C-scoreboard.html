The scoreboard

The centre line becomes a two-lane scoreboard, with your opponent's ink and lore on the top lane and yours on the bottom. Deck and discard move into the hand row, beside the hand. Both pile rows and both lore badges go.

A · Side dockB · Hand barC · Scoreboard

Try it

  • Drag a card onto your lane, the lower one. The whole lane is the drop target.
  • Click either lane for that player's inkwell panel.
  • Compare open ink across the two lanes: 6 of 6 against 1 of 6.
  • Switch to Today to compare, and Show space to see the boxes.

Gains

  • Both players' ink and lore fit in one strip about 64 px tall. This proposal gives back the most height.
  • You can see "they have 1 open, I have 6" without moving your eyes.
  • The lane is a wide, easy drop target on phones.
  • The hand row already had spare width either side, so deck and discard cost no height there.

Costs

  • Inking means dragging across your own field, which is the longest drag of the three.
  • Deck and discard sit at the end of the hand row, which leaves the hand about 120 px less width on each side.
  • Past about 20 ink the pips have to shrink to fit the lane.

Building it in app/index.html

  1. Turn #board-divider into a .band with two lanes, and render both ink counters and lore values into it.
  2. Remove both .pile-rows and both .lore-badges, and move .pile-cluster to the end of each .hand-wrap.
  3. Pips stay as one row per lane. Shrink --pw once the lane overflows.
  4. The panel anchors above or below the lane, which is today's logic.
  5. Phones use the same band with shorter labels and no lore buttons (tap the number instead).
