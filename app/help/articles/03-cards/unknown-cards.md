When you import a Duels.ink game, any card the log or replay never shows becomes an **unknown card**. An unknown card is a card back with no name. You can play on with unknown cards or replace them with real cards.

## Where they show up

- **Decks** hold the cards nobody drew or revealed yet. In the deck grid they show as a card back marked *(Unknown)*.
- **The opponent's hand** holds them when the game never revealed those cards.
- **Cards under a character** are unknown after a boost or shift in a text log.
- **Draw odds** groups them in one **Unknown** row, so you can see how many are left.

## Replace them

1. Open the deck. See [Look through your deck](help:inspect-deck).
2. Right-click an unknown card. **Replace this card** changes that one. **Replace all copies** turns every unknown card in the deck into the card you pick.
3. Press **Save Custom Order**.

You can also swap an unknown card in the other player's hand. Right-click it and choose **Swap Card**. On a phone, tap an unknown card in your hand and press **Swap**. See [Swap a card](help:swap-cards).

## Good to know

- No button fills the unknown cards from a decklist. Replace them by hand, or draw and play with them as they are.
- An unknown card has no cost or stats, so the curve and the odds of named cards leave it out.
- Saving the order adds a node to the Multiverse, so that turn keeps the filled-in deck.
