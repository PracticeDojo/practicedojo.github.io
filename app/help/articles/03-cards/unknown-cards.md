When you import a Duels.ink game, any card the log or replay never shows becomes an **unknown card**: a card back with no name. You can play on with them or replace them with real cards.

## Where they show up

- **Decks.** The cards nobody drew or revealed yet. In the deck grid they show as a card back marked *(Unknown)*.
- **The opponent's hand**, when the game never revealed those cards.
- **Cards under a character** after a boost or shift in a text log.
- **Draw odds** groups them in one **Unknown** row, so you can see how many are left.

## Replace them

1. Open the deck: see [Look through your deck](help:inspect-deck).
2. Right-click an unknown card. **Replace this card** changes that one; **Replace all copies** turns every unknown card in the deck into the card you pick.
3. Press **Save Custom Order**.

An unknown card in the other player's hand can be swapped: right-click it and choose **Swap Card**. On a phone, tap an unknown card in your hand and press **Swap** (see [Swap a card](help:swap-cards)).

## Good to know

- There's no button to fill the unknown cards from a decklist. Replace them by hand, or draw and play with them as they are.
- An unknown card has no cost or stats, so it stays out of the curve and the odds of named cards.
- Saving the order adds a node to the Multiverse, so the filled-in deck is kept with that turn.
