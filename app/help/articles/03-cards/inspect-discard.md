Every card that leaves play or is discarded goes to its owner's discard pile. You can read it at any time and take cards back out.

## Read the discard

:::desktop
Each half of the board has a **Discard** column: the cards listed newest first, with cost / strength / lore on the right. Past eight cards, **+N more** shows the rest. Point at a row to see the card in the preview.
:::

:::phone
The discard is the mini card on the player bar: it shows the top card and how many cards are in the pile.
:::

[Show me](show:discard)

## Open it in full

:::desktop
Click the magnifier in the **Discard** header, or right-click the column and choose **Inspect Discard Pile**.
:::

:::phone
Tap the magnifier on the discard's mini card.
:::

The pile opens as a grid of cards, oldest first.

## Bring a card back

:::desktop
Click or right-click a card in the column or the grid. Choose **Play Card to Field**, **Return to Hand**, **Move to Top of Deck** or **Move to Bottom of Deck**. You can also drag a row from the column onto the field, the hand or the deck.
:::

:::phone
Tap a card in the grid, or the top card on the bar, and pick one of the same moves. **Preview Card** shows it big.
:::

The grid stays open, so you can take out several cards.

## Good to know

- The Dojo doesn't check that a card is allowed to come back. You're the referee.
- **Play Card to Field** spends ink when the player has enough ready.
