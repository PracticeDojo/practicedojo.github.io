Cards on the board are small. The preview shows one big enough to read, with all its abilities.

## See a card big

:::desktop
Point at any card: on the board, in a hand, in the discard column, in the draw odds or in a deck or discard grid. It shows in the preview in the side rail, under the mini multiverse. The last card you pointed at stays there until you point at another.
:::

:::phone
Tap a card in play or in your hand. The card drawer opens with the card's full text and a small picture of it. Step to the next card with ‹ ›, or swipe sideways on the text.

For other cards (the discard, the other player's hand), tap the card and choose **Preview Card**. The ☰ side panel opens with the card in it.
:::

## Text mode

Turn on **Text** under **Card display** in Tweaks to show every card as text instead of art: the board, the hands, the grids and the preview. The text card has the cost (a gold disc if it can be inked, a dark hexagon if not), name, type line, every ability and the stats. See [Card size and Art / Text](help:card-display).

## Good to know

- Locations always show as text in the preview, so they aren't cropped.
- If a card's art can't load (offline, for example), the preview falls back to its text by itself.
- On a tablet, tapping a card in play opens the card drawer; a hand card's menu has **Preview Card**.
