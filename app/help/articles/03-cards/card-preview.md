Cards on the board are small. The preview shows one large enough to read, with all its abilities.

## See a card large

:::desktop
Point at any card on the board, in a hand, in the discard column, in the draw odds, or in a deck or discard grid. It shows in the preview in the side rail, under the mini multiverse. The last card you pointed at stays there until you point at another.
:::

:::phone
Tap a card in play or in your hand. The card drawer opens with the card's full text and a small picture of it. Step to the next card with ‹ ›, or swipe sideways on the text.

For other cards, such as those in the discard or the other player's hand, tap the card and choose **Preview Card**. The ☰ side panel opens with the card in it.
:::

## Text mode

Turn on **Text** under **Card display** in Tweaks to show every card as text instead of art. Text mode applies to the board, the hands, the grids and the preview. The text card shows the cost, name, type line, every ability and the stats. The cost sits in a winged hexagon if the card can be inked, or a plain hexagon if not. See [Card size and Art / Text](help:card-display).

## Good to know

- The preview always shows locations as text, so they aren't cropped.
- If a card's art can't load, for example offline, the preview shows its text instead.
- On a tablet, tapping a card in play opens the card drawer. A hand card's menu has **Preview Card**.
