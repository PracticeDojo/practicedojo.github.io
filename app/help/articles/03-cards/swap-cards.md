Swap a card for any other card to see how a different list plays, without building a new deck.

## Swap a card in your hand

:::desktop
Right-click the card in your hand and choose **Swap Card**.
:::

:::phone
Tap the card in your hand. In the card drawer, under **More**, press **Swap**.
:::

Type at least two letters of a card's name in **Search Replacement Card**, then click the card you want. It takes the old card's place in the hand straight away.

## Replace cards in the deck

1. Open the deck: see [Look through your deck](help:inspect-deck).
2. Right-click a card in the grid and choose **Replace this card**, or **Replace all copies** to change every copy of it in the deck.
3. Search and click the new card.
4. Press **Save Custom Order**. Until you do, nothing changes: **Cancel** throws the replacements away.

:::phone
On a phone, the menu for a card in the deck grid opens with a press and hold. Some phone browsers (Safari on iPhone) don't open it.
:::

## Good to know

- The search offers every card, not just your deck's inks. The Dojo doesn't check that the new card is legal in your deck, or how many copies you have.
- **Replace all copies** only changes the cards in the deck, not the copies in your hand, in play or in the discard.
- **Undo** takes back a hand swap.
- Swaps change this match only. To change a saved deck, edit it in the Library: see [Your decks](help:decks).
