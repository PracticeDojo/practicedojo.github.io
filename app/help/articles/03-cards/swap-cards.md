Swap a card for any other card to see how a different list plays, without building a new deck.

## Swap a card in your hand

:::desktop
Right-click the card in your hand and choose **Swap Card**.
:::

:::phone
Tap the card in your hand. In the card drawer, under **More**, press **Swap**.
:::

Type at least two letters of a card's name in **Search Replacement Card**, then click the card you want. It takes the old card's place in the hand at once.

## Replace cards in the deck

1. Open the deck. See [Look through your deck](help:inspect-deck).
2. Right-click a card in the grid and choose **Replace this card**. Choose **Replace all copies** to change every copy of it in the deck.
3. Search and click the new card.
4. Press **Save Custom Order**. Until you do, nothing changes, and **Cancel** throws the replacements away.

:::phone
On a phone, press and hold a card in the deck grid to open its menu. Some phone browsers don't open it, such as Safari on iPhone.
:::

## Good to know

- The search offers every card, not only those in your deck's inks. The Dojo doesn't check that the new card is legal in your deck, or how many copies you have.
- **Replace all copies** only changes the cards in the deck. Copies in your hand, in play or in the discard stay.
- **Undo** takes back a hand swap.
- Swaps change this match only. To change a saved deck, edit it in the Library. See [Your decks](help:decks).
