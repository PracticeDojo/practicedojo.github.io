The Dojo comes with a few decks and example games, so you can start without a list of your own.

## Play a built-in deck

On the home screen, under **On the table**, pick a deck chip. It goes to the first open seat (**You**, then **Opponent**). Press **Start match**.

[Show me](show:deck-chips)

The Library's **Decks** tab lists them on the **Built in** shelf, grouped by set, with **You** and **Opponent** to seat them. To make your own version, open its **⋯** menu and choose **Copy to my decks and edit**.

## Explore a demo

Under **Pick up again**, the demos are marked *Demo · opens as your own copy*. Press **Explore** to open one: a full game with its Multiverse, to step through and play from any turn.

[Show me](show:recent)

## Good to know

- A demo opens as a copy in your sessions. Change it as much as you like; the demo itself stays the same.
- Built-in decks are the same for everyone and can't be edited in place.
- **A decklist**, under the paste field, puts a built-in list in the field to try.
- An empty seat plays 60 random cards.
