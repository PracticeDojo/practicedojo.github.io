Keep the decks you play in **My decks**, so they're one tap away on the home screen.

## Paste a decklist

Paste a list into the field on the home screen, one card per line, like `4 Tinker Bell - Giant Fairy`. The readout under it says what it understood: the inks, and how many of the 60 cards it recognised (and which lines it skipped). Then choose:

- **Play it as you** or **Play against it** to seat it for the next match.
- **Save to My decks** to keep it.

[Show me](show:intake)

## Add a deck in the Library

Open **Library**, then **Decks**:

- **New deck**: type a name, paste the list, add notes if you like, and press **Save deck**.
- **Import .txt**: pick a text file with a decklist. It opens in the same form to check and save.
- **From a replay**: pick a Duels.ink replay file (.json, .replay or .replay.gz). The form fills with your exact 60 from that game.

[Show me](show:deck-new)

## Edit, seat or delete

Each deck has **You** and **Opponent** to seat it. Its **⋯** menu has **Edit**, **Share a link** and **Delete…**. Saving an edit updates a seat that holds that deck.

## Good to know

- The Dojo doesn't check deck rules. A list that isn't 60 cards plays with the cards it has, and the seat's count turns amber.
- If no card is recognised, the deck is 60 random cards.
- My decks live in this browser. Signing in keeps them in your account too: see [The Library](help:library).
