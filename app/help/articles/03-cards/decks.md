Keep the decks you play in **My decks**, so they're one tap away on the home screen.

## Paste a decklist

Paste a list into the field on the home screen, one card per line, like `4 Tinker Bell - Giant Fairy`. Duels.ink's own export works as it is, with the printing at the end of each line, like `4 Tinker Bell - Giant Fairy (1-193)`. The readout under the field shows the inks, how many of the 60 cards it recognised, and which lines it skipped. Then choose one:

- **Play it as you** or **Play against it** seats the deck for the next match.
- **Save to My decks** keeps it.

## Add a deck in the Library

Open **Library**, then **Decks**. There are three ways to add a deck:

- **New deck.** Type a name, paste the list, add notes if you like, and press **Save deck**.
- **Import .txt.** Pick a text file with a decklist. It opens in the same form to check and save.
- **From a replay.** Pick a Duels.ink replay file in .json, .replay or .replay.gz format. The form fills with your exact 60 from that game.

## Edit, seat or delete

Each deck has **You** and **Opponent** to seat it. Its **⋯** menu has **Edit**, **Share a link** and **Delete…**. Saving an edit updates any seat that holds that deck.

## Good to know

- The Dojo doesn't check deck rules. A list that isn't 60 cards plays with the cards it has, and the seat's count turns amber.
- If the Dojo recognises no card, the deck is 60 random cards.
- My decks live in this browser. Signing in also keeps them in your account. See [The Library](help:library).
