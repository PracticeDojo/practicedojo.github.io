Open a deck to see every card left in it, in draw order, and set up the draws you want to practise.

## Open the deck

:::desktop
Click the magnifier at the top right of the deck. Or right-click the deck and choose **Inspect Deck**. Clicking the deck itself draws a card.
:::

:::phone
Tap the ⋮ button on the deck in the player bar, then **Inspect Deck**. Tapping the deck itself draws a card.
:::

[Show me](show:deck)

The grid starts with the top card: the first card is the next one drawn. This works on both players' decks.

## Reorder it

1. Drag a card onto the place you want it. The card you dropped on moves along.
2. Press **Save Custom Order**. **Cancel** (or {key:Esc}) leaves the deck as it was.

Saving also adds a node called *Custom deck ordering* to the Multiverse, so you can come back to this order.

## Shuffle it

:::desktop
Click the shuffle button at the bottom right of the deck, or right-click the deck and choose **Shuffle Deck**.
:::

:::phone
Tap ⋮ on the deck, then **Shuffle Deck**.
:::

## Good to know

- The Dojo doesn't stop you from stacking a deck. You're the referee.
- To change which cards are in the deck, see [Swap a card](help:swap-cards).
- **Undo** takes back a shuffle or a saved order.
