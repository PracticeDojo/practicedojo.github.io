With **Auto-save every turn** on, each **End turn** adds a node to the Multiverse, so the whole game is saved turn by turn without you doing anything.

## Turn auto-save on or off

:::desktop
The **Auto-save every turn** switch sits at the foot of the side rail, above **Quest** and **End turn**.

[Show me](show:auto-save)
:::

:::phone
Open the ☰ menu. The **Auto-save every turn** switch is near the bottom, above **Quest** and **End turn**.

[Show me](show:menu)
:::

## What an auto-save keeps

When you end a turn, the next player readies and draws, and then the Dojo saves the board as a node. Its name says the turn and the player about to play: **Turn 5 - Player 2 Active** holds the board at the start of Player 2's turn 5. The node also keeps:

- the note written for the turn that just ended (see [Turn notes](help:turn-notes)),
- a **Turn recap** of that turn: what was drawn, inked, played, shifted, discarded, quested and banished,
- the cards behind the recap, in sections such as **Played**, **Inked** and **Drawn**.

The new node hangs off the node you were on, so the tree follows the line you're playing.

## Good to know

- With the switch off, ending a turn saves nothing. Save by hand with [Save current unfinished turn](help:save-unfinished-turn).
- The switch is on each time you open the Dojo; turning it off lasts until you reload.
- On turn 1, the node waits until Player 2 has settled their mulligan and drawn, so it holds the board Player 2 plays from.
- When a player reaches 20 lore, the Dojo saves a **Game Over** node even with auto-save off.
