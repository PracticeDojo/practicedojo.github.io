A node is one saved board. In the cards view each node is a small card; this is how to read it. (A phone opens the Multiverse on the plot; tap **Plot**, the grid of dots in the header, for the cards. See [Cards and plot](help:multiverse-views).)

## What's on a node

- **Tone:** a light card is Player 1's turn, a dark card Player 2's. The deck icon says the same.
- **Name:** an auto-save is called **Turn 16 - Player 2 Active**: the board at the start of that player's turn. A turn you saved yourself ends in **(unfinished)**.
- **Subline:** the turn, the lore (**P1: 10 - P2: 11**) and the time it was saved.
- **Note:** a dark box with the turn's note and its **Turn recap** (drew, inked, played…). Click it to edit it in the panel.
- **Played:** the cards played that turn.
- **The bin** deletes the node (with **Undo**).

## Which node is which

- **The node you're on** (your board came from it) has a coloured border and foot, and the path to it is highlighted. The panel calls it **· you are here**.
- **The selected node** (the one the panel shows) has four corner marks around it.
- **A "Left here" snapshot** has a dashed outline and a dashed line to its node. See ["Left here" snapshots](help:left-here).
- **A won branch** ends in a **Game Over** node with a trophy, a plate with the winner's lore and **Branch closed**, and a short bar after it.

## Every card section

Select a node to see all its card sections in the panel: **Played**, **Inked**, **Drawn**, **Discarded**, **Banished**, **Quested** and **Starting hand**. In **Starting hand**, cards that left the hand that turn are crossed out. To show every section inside every node, turn on **Show in nodes** under **Turn recap** in the panel.

## Good to know

- The name and the note are yours to change: see [The panel](help:multiverse-panel).
- On an auto-save, **Turn recap** lists the turn that just ended, not the one the board is about to play.
