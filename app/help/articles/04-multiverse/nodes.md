A node is one saved board. In the cards view each node is a small card. This article explains how to read it. A phone opens the Multiverse on the plot, so tap **Plot** in the header for the cards. See [Cards and plot](help:multiverse-views).

## What's on a node

- **Tone.** A light card is Player 1's turn, and a dark card is Player 2's. The deck icon shows the same.
- **Name.** An auto-save is named like **Turn 16 - Player 2 Active**, which is the board at the start of that player's turn. The name of a turn you saved yourself ends in **(unfinished)**.
- **Subline.** It gives the turn, the lore, like **P1: 10 - P2: 11**, and the time the node was saved.
- **Note.** A dark box holds the turn's note and its **Turn recap** of what was drawn, inked and played. Click it to edit it in the panel.
- **Played.** It lists the cards played that turn.
- **The bin.** It deletes the node, and **Undo** brings it back.

## Which node is which

- **The node you're on** is the one your board came from. It has a coloured border and foot, and the path to it is highlighted. The panel calls it **· you are here**.
- **The selected node** is the one the panel shows. It has four corner marks around it.
- **A "Left here" snapshot** has a dashed outline and a dashed line to its node. See ["Left here" snapshots](help:left-here).
- **A won branch** ends in a **Game Over** node with a trophy, a plate with the winner's lore and **Branch closed**, and a short bar after it.

## Every card section

Select a node to see all its card sections in the panel. They are **Played**, **Inked**, **Drawn**, **Discarded**, **Banished**, **Quested** and **Starting hand**. In **Starting hand**, cards that left the hand that turn are crossed out. To show every section inside every node, turn on **Show in nodes** under **Turn recap** in the panel.

## Good to know

- You can change the name and the note. See [The panel](help:multiverse-panel).
- On an auto-save, **Turn recap** lists what was done in that node's turn. A turn still being played has no recap yet.
