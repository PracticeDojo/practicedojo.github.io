The Multiverse draws the tree in two views. The cards view shows full nodes you can read. The plot shows every saved turn as a dot, so you can see a whole game at once.

## Switch between cards and plot

:::desktop
Click **Plot** in the Multiverse header. It stays dark while the plot is on. Click it again for the cards. The Dojo remembers your choice.
:::

:::phone
Tap **Plot** in the Multiverse header, left of ✕, to switch to the cards while the Multiverse is open. Its icon is a grid of dots. A phone always opens the Multiverse on the plot, because the cards are too wide for the screen.
:::

## Read the plot

- Each saved turn is a dot in its deck's two inks. Player 1's dots are solid and Player 2's are striped. The turn number sits under each dot.
- A ruler along the top gives each column's turn and player, **P1** or **P2**.
- Lines run from each turn to the next. The path to the node you're on is highlighted. "Left here" snapshots hang on dashed lines.
- **You are here** is a ring around the dot your board came from.
- **Cursor** is a crosshair on the selected dot. Its tag gives the turn, player and lore, like **T12 · P1 · 7–6**.
- **20 lore · branch closed** marks a won branch. It ends in a square dot and a bar, with the winner's lore and name.
- **Left here** is a dashed ring.

A key names all of these. It runs along the foot of the canvas on desktop and along its top on a phone.

## Good to know

- Click or tap a dot to select it, as with a card. The panel shows it.
- On desktop the plot opens fitted to the whole game. A phone opens it close up, on the cursor. The zoom out thumb key shows the whole game. See [Moving around](help:multiverse-navigate).
- The cards show more of each turn, and the plot shows the shape of the game.
