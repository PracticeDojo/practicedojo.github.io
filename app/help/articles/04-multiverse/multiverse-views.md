The Multiverse draws the tree two ways: as cards, full nodes you can read, or as the plot, where every saved turn is a dot so you can see a whole game at once.

## Switch between cards and plot

:::desktop
Click **Plot** in the Multiverse header; it stays dark while the plot is on. Click it again for the cards.
:::

:::phone
Tap **Plot** (the grid of dots) in the Multiverse header, left of ✕. A phone opens on the plot until you pick a view.
:::

The Dojo remembers your choice.

## Read the plot

- Each saved turn is a dot in its deck's two inks: **Player 1 solid, Player 2 striped**. Its turn number sits under it.
- A ruler along the top gives the turn and whose step each column is (**P1**, **P2**).
- Lines run from each turn to the next. The path to the node you're on is highlighted; "Left here" snapshots hang on dashed lines.
- **You are here:** a ring around the dot your board came from.
- **Cursor:** a crosshair on the selected dot, with a tag like **T12 · P1 · 7–6** (turn, player, lore).
- **20 lore · branch closed:** a won branch ends in a square dot, a bar, the winner's lore and who won.
- **Left here:** a dashed ring.

A key names all of these: along the foot of the canvas on desktop, along its top on a phone.

## Good to know

- Click or tap a dot to select it, as with a card. The panel shows it.
- On desktop the plot opens fitted to the whole game. A phone opens it up close, on the cursor; the zoom out thumb key shows the whole game (see [Moving around](help:multiverse-navigate)).
- The cards show more of each turn; the plot shows the shape of the game.
