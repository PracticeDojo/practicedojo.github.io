A short debrief after each game, and a table of your record per matchup, show you which matchup to practise and what to work on.

## Debrief a game

When a player reaches 20 lore, press **Debrief**. At any other time, open **Multiverse** and press **Debrief this game** at the top of the panel.

[Show me](show:debrief-open)

- **You played**: which side was you. For an imported Duels.ink game, pick your own name.
- **Result**: won or lost. A game with no result is saved, but Matchups doesn't count it.
- **Decided on turn**: the turn the game turned, if one did.
- **What went wrong**: tap every tag that applies. The tags are decisions (*Mulligan*, *Inking*, *Quest vs challenge*…), not a grade.
- **One lesson**: one sentence you'd tell yourself before the next game in this matchup.

**Save debrief** keeps it with the session. Open it again to change it.

## See your matchups

On the home screen, open **Sessions** and press **Matchups**.

[Show me](show:lib-matchups)

Each row is a matchup, your inks against theirs, with your wins and losses, the win rate, your most common tag and your latest lesson. The weakest matchup comes first: that's the one to practise next. Under five games, the rate is mostly luck, and the row says so.

## Good to know

- Matchups counts the sessions on this device. Decks in the same two inks share a row.
- A debrief belongs to its session, so it's part of the session's file, its share links and its account copy.
- Debriefing your real games (imported from Duels.ink) gives you the most honest record.
