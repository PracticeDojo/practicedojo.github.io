Open the link in your browser. You don't need an account, and the Dojo saves nothing until you choose to keep it.

## A shared session

The match opens on the board with a bar at the top. The bar shows **Shared**, the title, **not saved yet** and **Save a copy**.

[Show me](show:shared-banner)

Look around, play on and open the Multiverse. While the bar shows, nothing goes into your library. To keep the match, press **Save a copy**. **Save** in the top bar does the same. The match becomes your own session on this device, under the same title, and the Dojo saves it from then on like any other.

If you open something else instead, or reload the page, your changes to the shared match are gone. A reload opens the link again from the start.

## A shared deck

The Dojo opens the home screen with the deck in the paste field, marked **Shared deck** with its name. Press **Play it as you** or **Play against it** to seat it, or **Save to My decks** to keep it.

[Show me](show:intake)

## If it doesn't open

- **Link not recognised** means part of the link is missing. Ask for it again, or check that you copied all of it.
- **This share is no longer available** means the person who shared it deleted it.
- **Couldn't open the link** means the Dojo couldn't reach the sharing service. Check your connection and try again.

## Good to know

- Your copy is yours. Changing it never changes the link or the sharer's session.
- A shared link is a snapshot of the moment it was made. If the sharer keeps playing, ask for a new link.
