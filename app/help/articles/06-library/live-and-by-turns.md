There's nothing to switch: a table is **live** while you both have it open, and goes **by turns** when one of you leaves. Close the tab or come back whenever you like.

## Whose turn it is

Only the player whose turn it is changes the board. The other player watches: they can look at cards, the discard piles, the log and the Multiverse, but card actions, **Undo** and **End turn** wait for their turn. The turn chip says **You** or your opponent's name.

**End turn** hands the board over. If you ended your turn too soon, ask your opponent to press **Undo**: it gives the turn back to you.

## Live

When you're both here, every move shows on the other screen within a second, and the table chip reads *Live with Sam*.

## By turns

Play your turn and press **End turn**. The table keeps it, and the bar at the top says your opponent plays next. Send them the link (**Copy link**) as a nudge.

When it's your turn, the table shows **Your turn** in *Pick up again* and in the Library. Open it and play. If the tab is open in the background when your turn arrives, its title starts with ● Your turn.

You can play your turn offline. Your moves are kept on this device and sent when you're back online.

## Hands

Your own hand is face up and your opponent's is face down, wherever it sits on the screen. If you **Reveal** their hand or look through their deck, the log says so and they see it. It's a practice table between friends, not a secret: both browsers hold the whole board.
