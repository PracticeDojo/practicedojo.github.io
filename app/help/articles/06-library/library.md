The Library holds every session and deck you keep, sorted onto shelves by where each one lives.

## Open the Library

On the home screen, press **Library** at the top, or **All sessions** in **Pick up again**. The figures on the button count your sessions on this device and, signed in, in your account. {key:Esc} or **×** closes it.

[Show me](show:library-button)

From a match, leave to the home screen first (see [The screen at a glance](help:screen-at-a-glance)).

## Three tabs

- **Sessions**: every match you've kept. **Open** loads one (**Back to it** for the match on the board). Its **⋯** menu has **Rename**, **Share a link**, **Duplicate**, **Export file (.dojo.json.gz)** and **Delete…**. **Import file** adds a session file: see [Export and import session files](help:files).
- **Decks**: your decks and the **Built in** ones. **You** and **Opponent** seat a deck for the next match. **New deck**, **Import .txt** and **From a replay** add one. See [Your decks](help:decks).
- **Links**: the share links you made, under **Public links**. See [Share a deck or a session](help:share-links).

## The shelves

Signed out, your sessions and decks sit on **On this device**, with an empty **Your account** shelf that offers **Sign in**. Signed in, they sort into **In your account + this device**, **Only on this device** and **Only in your account**. Each shelf says in one line what that means.

A row only adds a note when it's out of step, like **Changed here since your last Save**. See [What goes to your account](help:account-sync).

:::phone
Each row's buttons sit at the foot of its card.
:::

## Good to know

- **Delete…** on a session or deck can't be undone. Export a session first if you might want it back.
- **On this device** means this browser only: clearing its site data deletes them. See [Where your things are kept](help:storage).
