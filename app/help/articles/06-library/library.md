The Library holds every session and deck you keep, sorted onto shelves by where each one lives.

## Open the Library

On the home screen, press **Library** at the top, or **All sessions** in **Pick up again**. The figures on the button count your sessions on this device and, when you're signed in, in your account. {key:Esc} or **×** closes it.

[Show me](show:library-button)

From a match, leave to the home screen first. See [The screen at a glance](help:screen-at-a-glance).

## The tabs

- **Sessions** lists every match you've kept. **Open** loads one, and the match on the board shows **Back to it** instead. Its **⋯** menu has **Rename**, **Share a link**, **Duplicate**, **Export file (.dojo.json.gz)** and **Delete…**. **Import file** adds a session file. See [Export and import session files](help:files).
- **Decks** lists your decks and the **Built in** ones. **You** and **Opponent** seat a deck for the next match. **New deck**, **Import .txt** and **From a replay** add one. See [Your decks](help:decks).
- **Links** lists the share links you made, under **Public links**. See [Share a deck or a session](help:share-links).

## Find a session or a deck

**Sessions** has **Find a session or a card in it** at the top, and **Decks** has **Find a deck or a card in one**. Type a name or a card. The search allows typos and missing letters, so `lvthn` finds The Leviathan. A row found by a card names that card. The ink buttons narrow the list. One ink shows what uses it, and two show that exact pair. A session matches when either of its decks does. The Dojo reads a session's cards the first time you search. While you search, only the shelves with matches show.

## The shelves

When you're signed out, your sessions and decks sit on **On this device**, with an empty **Your account** shelf that offers **Sign in**. When you're signed in, they sort into **In your account + this device**, **Only on this device** and **Only in your account**. Each shelf says in one line what that means.

The **Built in** shelf folds away with the arrow on its heading, and stays folded next time. Searching shows it again.

A row adds a note only when it's out of step, like **Changed here since your last Save**. See [What goes to your account](help:account-sync).

:::phone
Each row's buttons sit at the foot of its card.
:::

## Good to know

- You can't undo **Delete…** on a session or deck. Export a session first if you might want it back.
- **On this device** means this browser only. Clearing its site data deletes everything on that shelf. See [Where your things are kept](help:storage).
