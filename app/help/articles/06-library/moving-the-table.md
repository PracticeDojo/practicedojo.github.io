At a table, the Multiverse belongs to both of you, so moving the board to another node is something you agree on.

## Ask

Pick a node the way you'd load it alone: **Play from here** in the Multiverse panel, a double-click, or a node in the mini multiverse. Instead of loading, the bar at the top says *Waiting for Sam to go to "Turn 6 – Amber"*, with **Cancel**. Your board stays as it is until they answer.

You can ask on either player's turn.

## Answer

When your opponent asks, the bar says *Sam wants to go to "Turn 6 – Amber"*. Press **Go there** and both boards move. Press **Stay** and the table stays where it is.

If you're playing by turns, the question waits on the table until the other player next opens it.

## Nothing is lost

The board you leave is kept as a ["Left here" snapshot](help:left-here) when it hasn't been saved, as it is when you jump alone. If you were in the middle of a turn when the other player moved the table, your board is kept the same way.

To try a line on your own without moving the table, use **Take a copy** from the table chip.
