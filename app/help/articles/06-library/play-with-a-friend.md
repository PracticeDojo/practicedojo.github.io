A table is a match two people share. You each take one side, and the board and its whole Multiverse belong to both of you. Nobody needs an account.

## Set a table

:::desktop
Press the two-people button next to the share button in the top bar.

[Show me](show:play-with-a-friend)
:::

:::phone
Tap **⋯** in the top bar, then **Play with a friend**.
:::

Type the name your opponent will see, press **Next**, and choose your side (**P1** or **P2**). The **Table ready** box shows a link like `practicedojo.win/app/?t=k3J9xQ2mPa`. Press **Copy link** and send it to your opponent.

The match you started from stays in your library as it was. The table is a new session, marked with whose turn it is.

## Join a table

Open the link you were sent. Type your name and press **Join table**: you take the free side. Whoever opens the link first gets the seat; once both seats are taken, the link only works for the two of you.

## The table chip

At a table, a chip next to the session title says who's there and whose turn it is: *Live with Sam · Your turn*, *Sam is away · Sam's turn*, or *Waiting for a player*. On a phone it's just its dot: filled when you're both here, hollow when your opponent is away.

Press it for the seats, **Copy link**, **Take a copy** (your own session to try things in, without moving the table), and **Leave the table**. Leaving keeps your copy as your own session. The last one to leave closes the table.

## On another device

Your seat belongs to this browser. To play the same table on another device, [sign in](help:sign-in) here first, then sign in there and open the link.
