A share link sends a deck, or a whole match with its Multiverse, to anyone. They open it in their browser, and neither of you needs an account.

## Share the match on the board

:::desktop
Press the share button next to **Save** in the top bar.

[Show me](show:share)
:::

:::phone
Tap **⋯** in the top bar, then **Share link**.
:::

## Share from the Library

- **A session.** In **Sessions**, open its **⋯** and choose **Share a link**.
- **A deck.** In **Decks**, open its **⋯** and choose **Share a link**. You can share built-in decks too.

## Copy the link

When it's ready, the **Link ready** box shows a short link like `practicedojo.win/app/?s=k3J9xQ2mPa`. Press **Copy link**, then paste it wherever you like.

## See and delete your links

The Library's **Links** tab lists your links under **Public links**. Press **Copy link** to copy one again. Its **⋯** has **Open in a new tab** and **Delete link**. A deleted link stops working for everyone, and opens as "This share is no longer available".

## Good to know

- A link is a snapshot. Later changes aren't in it, so share again to send a newer version.
- Anyone who has the link can open it. A session link carries everything in the session, including your notes.
- Making a link needs a connection. The first time, the Dojo gives this browser its own sharing identity without a sign-in screen.
- When you're signed out, the **Links** tab lists the links made from this browser. Clearing its site data loses that list, but the links keep working. When you're signed in, links belong to your account. Links this browser made before you signed in move to your account, unless that Discord or Google account was already a Dojo account.
- The limits are 20 links a day, 100 in all, and sessions up to 10 MB compressed. See [Share link limits](help:share-limits).
