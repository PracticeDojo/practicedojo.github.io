A session is one match with its whole Multiverse. The Dojo keeps every session on this device as you play, so you never lose a match by closing the tab.

## What makes a session

A new session starts when you do one of these:

- press **Start match** on the home screen.
- open a saved session file.
- study a Duels.ink log or replay.
- open a demo, which opens as your own copy.

The Dojo names it from the date and the decks, like *Oct 13 · Steelsong Tempo vs Ruby/Sapphire*. It uses a deck's name, or its inks when the deck has no name. You can rename it. See [Save, save as copy, rename](help:save-and-rename).

## Saving happens by itself

The Dojo saves every change to this device about a second after you make it, and again when you switch tabs or close the page. You don't have to press **Save**. Leaving to the home screen also keeps the match.

## Pick up again

The home screen's **Pick up again** box lists **Your sessions** first. The last session comes first with **Resume**, then your most recent ones. The **Demos** come after them with **Explore**. The match on the board says **Back to it**. **All sessions** opens the [Library](help:library) with every session you have.

**Find a session or a card in it** searches all your sessions and the demos, by title or by a card in either deck. The search allows typos and missing letters, so `lvthn` finds The Leviathan. A row found by a card names that card. The ink buttons narrow the list. One ink shows what uses it, and two show that exact pair. The arrow on **Demos** folds them away, and they stay folded next time.

## Good to know

- Sessions live in this browser on this device. Clearing the site's data or closing a private window deletes them, and another browser or device doesn't have them. Export a file to keep a backup, or [sign in](help:account) to keep them in an account. See [Where your things are kept](help:storage).
- **Auto-save every turn** does something else. It saves a node in the [Multiverse](help:auto-save) at each turn. The switch is in the side rail, or the ☰ menu on a phone.
- If the Dojo can't save to this device, it tells you once with "Couldn't save to this device. Export the session to keep it."
