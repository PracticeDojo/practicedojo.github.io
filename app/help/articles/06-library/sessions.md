A session is one match with its whole Multiverse. The Dojo keeps every session on this device as you play, so you never lose a match by closing the tab.

## What makes a session

A new session starts when you:

- press **Start match** on the home screen,
- open a saved session file,
- study a Duels.ink log or replay,
- open a demo (it opens as your own copy).

It gets a name from the date and the decks, like *Oct 13 · Steelsong Tempo vs Ruby/Sapphire*: a deck's name, or its inks when it has no name. You can rename it: see [Save, save as copy, rename](help:save-and-rename).

## Saving happens by itself

Every change you make is saved to this device about a second later, and again when you switch tabs or close the page. You don't have to press **Save**. Leaving to the home screen keeps the match too.

## Pick up again

The home screen's **Pick up again** box lists **Your sessions**, the last one first (**Resume**), then your most recent ones, and then the **Demos** (**Explore**). The match on the board says **Back to it**. **All sessions** opens the [Library](help:library) with every session you have.

**Find a session or a card in it** searches all your sessions and the demos, by title or by a card in either deck. Type in the search to find one by its name or by a card in it, with typos and missing letters (`lvthn` finds The Leviathan); a row found by a card says which. The ink buttons narrow the list: one ink shows what uses it, two show that exact pair. The arrow on **Demos** folds them away, and they stay folded next time.

## Good to know

- Sessions live in this browser on this device. Clearing the site's data or closing a private window deletes them, and another browser or device doesn't have them. Export a file to keep a backup, or [sign in](help:account) to keep them in an account. See [Where your things are kept](help:storage).
- **Auto-save every turn** (in the side rail, or the ☰ menu on a phone) is different: it saves a node in the [Multiverse](help:auto-save) at each turn.
- If the Dojo can't save to this device, it tells you once: "Couldn't save to this device. Export the session to keep it."
