A Duels.ink replay file records the game exactly. It's the best way to bring a game in: your own deck comes through card for card, in order.

## Import it

1. Download the replay from Duels.ink. It usually ends in **.replay.gz**; there's no need to unzip it.
2. On the home screen, press **Choose a file** and pick it, or drop the file anywhere on the page.
3. The readout shows **Duels.ink replay**, the two players and the number of turns.
4. Press **Study this game**.

[Show me](show:intake-file)

You can also open **Multiverse**, press **Log**, choose the file and press **Import**.

## Play your deck from it

The readout's **Play my deck from it** seats the deck you played as **You**, ready for a new match. To keep that deck, use **From a replay** in the Library's **Decks** tab: see [Your decks](help:decks).

## Good to know

- The players keep their Duels.ink names, and each keeps their Duels.ink seat: if you were the second player, you're Player 2.
- Your deck is exact, in its real order. The opponent's hidden cards stay unknown until they show them: see [What an import knows and doesn't](help:hidden-information).
- Only replay files carry a decklist. A text log can't give you **Play my deck from it**.
