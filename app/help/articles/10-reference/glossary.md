The words this manual uses: first the Dojo's own, then Lorcana's.

## Dojo words

- **Session.** One match and everything saved from it: the board, its Multiverse and its history. It saves itself as you play. [Sessions](help:sessions)
- **Multiverse.** The tree of every saved turn in a session. [The Multiverse](help:multiverse)
- **Node.** One saved board in the Multiverse: whose turn, the lore, a note and what happened. [Reading a node](help:nodes)
- **Line.** A run of turns, one after another, from the start to where it ends.
- **Branch.** A line that splits off an earlier node, when you play from there and do something different. [Branching](help:branches)
- **The line you're on.** The line that leads to the board in front of you. The Dojo marks it, and the node you're on, in the signal colour.
- **Auto-save.** With **Auto-save every turn** on, ending a turn saves the board as a node. [Every turn is saved](help:auto-save)
- **Left here.** A dashed snapshot of a board you jumped away from before saving it. [Left here snapshots](help:left-here)
- **Library.** Your sessions, decks and links, on shelves by where they're kept: this device, your account, built in, public links. [The Library](help:library)
- **Share link.** A short link to a frozen copy of a deck or a session. Anyone with it can open it. [Share a deck or a session](help:share-links)
- **Practice board.** The board a guided tour runs on. Nothing on it is saved, and your own match is kept as it was. [Guided tours](help:tours)
- **Unknown card.** A card an imported game never showed. It reads **Unknown card** until you fill it in. [Unknown cards](help:unknown-cards)

## Lorcana words

- **Ink, inkwell.** Cards put face down to pay for other cards. Each ink pays 1 once a turn. [Ink a card](help:ink-a-card)
- **Ready, exert.** A ready card stands upright. Exerting turns it sideways, to quest, challenge or pay for an ability. [Act on a card](help:card-actions)
- **Quest.** Exert a character to gain its lore. [Quest and lore](help:quest)
- **Lore.** The score. The first player to 20 wins. [Winning](help:winning)
- **Challenge.** Exert your character to fight an exerted opposing character (or a location); each deals damage equal to its strength. [Challenge](help:challenge)
- **Banish.** Put a card from play into its owner's discard, for example when its damage reaches its willpower.
- **Drying.** A character played this turn can't quest or challenge yet. The board marks it **NEW**.
- **Shift.** Play a character on top of another version of it, for its shift cost. The card underneath stays under it. [Locations, shift and cards under](help:locations-and-stacks)
- **Singer, song.** A character can exert to sing a song instead of paying its ink. The Dojo doesn't track singing: exert the singer and play the song yourself.
- **Location.** A card your characters can move to. It has willpower and can give lore. [Locations](help:locations-and-stacks)

The Dojo doesn't enforce Lorcana's rules. You're the referee.
