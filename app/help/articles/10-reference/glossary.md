This page defines the words the manual uses, first the Dojo's own and then Lorcana's.

## Dojo words

- **Session.** One match and everything saved from it, with the board, its Multiverse and its history. The Dojo saves it as you play. [Sessions](help:sessions)
- **Multiverse.** The tree of every saved turn in a session. [The Multiverse](help:multiverse)
- **Node.** One saved board in the Multiverse. It shows whose turn it is, the lore, a note and what happened. [Reading a node](help:nodes)
- **Line.** A run of turns, one after another, from the start to where it ends.
- **Branch.** A line that splits off an earlier node, when you play from there and do something different. [Branching](help:branches)
- **The line you're on.** The line that leads to the board in front of you. The Dojo marks it, and the node you're on, in the signal colour.
- **Auto-save.** With **Auto-save every turn** on, every turn gets a node. The node holds the board the turn starts from, and gets the turn's note and recap once it ends. [Every turn is saved](help:auto-save)
- **Left here.** A dashed snapshot of a board you jumped away from before saving it. [Left here snapshots](help:left-here)
- **Library.** Your sessions, decks and links, on shelves by where they're kept. The shelves are this device, your account, built in and public links. [The Library](help:library)
- **Share link.** A short link to a frozen copy of a deck or a session. Anyone with the link can open it. [Share a deck or a session](help:share-links)
- **Rules engine.** An optional beta helper that knows Lorcana's rules. It marks legal moves, suggests plays, plays a seat as a bot and runs What-if. It never blocks a move. [The rules engine](help:rules-engine)
- **Proposed plays.** The best lines for the rest of the turn that **Suggest** finds. Each line shows the lore it gains and why it scores. [Proposed plays](help:proposed-plays)
- **What-if.** A lost game played out again from your turns before the loss, looking for a line that would have won. [What-if](help:what-if)
- **Practice board.** The board a guided tour runs on. Nothing on it is saved, and your own match stays as it was. [Guided tours](help:tours)
- **Unknown card.** A card an imported game never showed. It reads **Unknown card** until you fill it in. [Unknown cards](help:unknown-cards)

## Lorcana words

- **Ink, inkwell.** Cards put face down to pay for other cards. Each ink pays 1, once a turn. [Ink a card](help:ink-a-card)
- **Ready, exert.** A ready card stands upright. Exerting turns it sideways, to quest, challenge or pay for an ability. [Act on a card](help:card-actions)
- **Quest.** Exert a character to gain its lore. [Quest and lore](help:quest)
- **Lore.** The score. The first player to reach 20 wins. [Winning](help:winning)
- **Challenge.** Exert your character to fight an exerted opposing character or a location. Each card deals damage equal to its strength to the other. [Challenge](help:challenge)
- **Banish.** Put a card from play into its owner's discard, for example when its damage reaches its willpower.
- **Drying.** A character played this turn can't quest or challenge yet. The board marks it **NEW**.
- **Shift.** Play a character on top of another version of it, for its shift cost. The card underneath stays under it. [Locations, shift and cards under](help:locations-and-stacks)
- **Singer, song.** A character can exert to sing a song instead of paying its ink. The Dojo doesn't track singing, so exert the singer and play the song yourself.
- **Location.** A card your characters can move to. It has willpower and can give lore. [Locations](help:locations-and-stacks)

The Dojo doesn't enforce Lorcana's rules. You're the referee.
