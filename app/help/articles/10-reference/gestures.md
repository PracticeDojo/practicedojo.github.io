Everything you can do with a finger. Most of it is a tap that opens the card drawer; the rest are holds, swipes and drags.

## Cards

| Gesture | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| {gesture:tap} | A card in play | Opens the card drawer: damage, **Challenge**, **Quest**, **Exert**, **Banish** and the moves |
| {gesture:tap} | A card in your hand (phone) | Opens the card drawer for it: **Play**, **Ink**, **Discard**, and more |
| {gesture:swipe} | Sideways on the card text in the drawer | The next card (swipe left) or the previous one (swipe right) |
| {gesture:tap} | Outside the drawer | Closes it |
| {gesture:drag} | A card from your hand, up | Onto the field to play it, onto the ink count to ink it, onto a matching character to shift it |
| {gesture:drag} | Any card you can pick up | Onto the discard, the deck or a hand to move it there |

:::desktop touch
On a tablet, a tap on a card in your hand opens its menu instead of the drawer.
:::

## Challenge

| Gesture | What it does |
|---|---|
| {gesture:tap} **Challenge** | Turns challenge mode on |
| {gesture:tap} your character, then an opponent's card | Challenges it |
| {gesture:drag} your character onto an opponent's card | Challenges it too |

## The board

| Gesture | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| {gesture:long-press} | The empty battlefield, about half a second | Opens **Turn N notes** for the active player |
| {gesture:tap} | The ink count | Opens the inkwell sheet: spend, ready, **Ready all**, each ink card |
| {gesture:tap} | The deck | Draws a card |
| {gesture:tap} | **⋮** on the deck | Deck actions: inspect, shuffle |
| {gesture:tap} | The magnifier on the discard | Opens the discard |
| {gesture:tap} | **Hold to reveal hand** | Shows the other player's hand; tap again to hide it |
| {gesture:tap} | **−** / **+** by the lore | Changes the lore by one |
| {gesture:tap} | **Quest** / **End turn** by your hand | Quest with every ready character / end the turn |

Moving your finger cancels a long press, so scrolling never opens the notes.

## Mulligan

| Gesture | What it does |
|---|---|
| {gesture:tap} a card | Marks it to go to the bottom (tap again to keep it) |
| {gesture:long-press} a card | Shows it large with its name, cost and type. It stays when you lift your finger |
| {gesture:tap} anywhere | Closes the large card. That tap marks nothing |

## Multiverse

| Gesture | What it does |
|---|---|
| {gesture:tap} a node or dot | Selects it; the panel shows it |
| {gesture:tap} **Play from here** | Loads the selected node |
| {gesture:drag} the canvas | Pans |
| {gesture:pinch} | Zooms in or out |
| {gesture:tap} a thumb key | ◀ ▶ the turn before / after, ▲ ▼ another line, ◎ back to where you are, zoom out to see the whole game (plot only) |
| {gesture:long-press} an arrow key | Keeps stepping, like holding a key |

## Menus

- {gesture:tap} **☰** for the side rail: the players, the game log and **Auto-save every turn**.
- {gesture:tap} **⋯** for **Save**, **Save as copy**, **Share link**, **Tweaks** and **Leave to the dojo**. Tap outside to close it.
