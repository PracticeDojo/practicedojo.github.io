Your sessions and decks are saved in this browser, on this device. There's no Dojo server holding them unless you sign in.

## What deletes them

- **Clearing this site's data** (cookies and site data, or browsing data) deletes every session and deck saved here.
- **Another browser or device** has its own, separate library. Chrome and Firefox on the same computer don't share one.
- **A private window** may not keep anything after it closes, or may not let the Dojo save at all. The Library then says *This browser isn't letting the Dojo keep a library (private window?).*
- **The browser itself** may clear storage when the device runs short of space. When you save, the Dojo asks the browser to keep its storage, but the browser decides.

If a save fails, the Dojo says *Couldn't save to this device. Export the session to keep it.*

## How to keep them

- **Export a file.** In the Library, open a session's **⋯** menu and choose **Export file (.dojo.json.gz)**. Keep the file anywhere; **Import file** opens it again. See [Export and import session files](help:files).
- **Sign in.** Decks follow your account by themselves; a session goes to your account when you press **Save**. See [Why sign in](help:account).

## Good to know

- The Library sorts everything by where it lives: *On this device*, *Only on this device*, *In your account + this device*.
- Your share links belong to this browser too. Clear its data and the links keep working for everyone, but you can no longer list or delete them, unless you signed in first.
- Your **Tweaks** choices are kept in this browser as well.
