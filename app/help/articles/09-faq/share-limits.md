Share links are free and need no account, so the Dojo keeps a few limits to stay that way. Opening a link has no limits.

## The limits

- **20 links a day and 100 in total**, per browser (or per account once you sign in). Both count the links you still have: deleting old ones in **Library → Links** frees room. The day is the last 24 hours.
- **10 MB per session**, compressed. A deck link is just the list.
- **Shared storage.** When the Dojo's shared storage fills up, sharing sessions pauses for everyone. Deck links still work.

## What the messages mean

- *You've reached the sharing limit: 20 links a day and 100 in total.* Delete links you no longer need, or wait.
- *This session is N MB compressed, and shared sessions can be up to 10 MB.* Delete auto-saves or dead-end branches in the Multiverse, then share again.
- *Sharing sessions is paused because the Dojo's shared storage is full.* Nothing you can fix; share the deck, or send the file instead ([Export and import session files](help:files)).
- *Too many new sharers from this network in the last hour.* The first link from a browser sets it up for sharing, and that is limited per network. Try again later.
- *This browser can't compress session files, so it can't share them.* Use a current browser.
- *Couldn't reach the sharing service.* Check your connection.
- **This share is no longer available**: the person who made the link deleted it.

## Good to know

- A link is a snapshot. Later changes to your session aren't in it; share again to send them.
