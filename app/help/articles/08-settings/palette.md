**Player palette** in [Tweaks](help:tweaks) sets how the Dojo tells the two players apart by colour.

## Choose a palette

[Show me](show:tweak-palette)

- **Competition** (the default): each side is shaded in its own deck's two inks, on the board, the player rows, the lore and the Multiverse nodes. Player 1's mark is split in two; Player 2's is striped like a barber pole, so a mirror match still reads. Under the switch, a P1 and a P2 icon show the inks it picked; hover one to see their names. Before both decks are known, it uses Modern's colours.
- **Modern**: two fixed colours, olive for Player 1 and teal for Player 2, whatever the decks.
- **Mono**: grey only. There's no colour anywhere, card pictures included. Player 1 is light and Player 2 is heavy.

## Good to know

- The palette only changes the players' colours. The light or dark look is the [Theme](help:theme).
- It's kept in this browser.
