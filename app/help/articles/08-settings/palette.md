**Player palette** in [Tweaks](help:tweaks) sets how the Dojo tells the two players apart by colour.

## Choose a palette

[Show me](show:tweak-palette)

- **Competition** is the default. It shades each side in its own deck's two inks, on the board, the player rows, the lore and the Multiverse nodes. Player 1's mark is split in two, and Player 2's is striped like a barber pole, so you can tell the players apart in a mirror match. Under the switch, a P1 and a P2 icon show the inks it picked. Hover one to see their names. Before both decks are known, it uses Modern's colours.
- **Modern** uses two fixed colours, olive for Player 1 and teal for Player 2, whatever the decks.
- **Mono** uses grey only. There's no colour anywhere, card pictures included. Player 1 is light and Player 2 is heavy.

## Good to know

- The palette only changes the players' colours. The [Theme](help:theme) sets the light or dark look.
- This browser keeps the setting.
