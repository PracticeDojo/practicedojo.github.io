Tweaks is where the Dojo's settings live: how it looks, how big the cards are, and how much it tells you about a challenge.

## Open Tweaks

:::desktop
Press the sliders button at the right end of the top bar. Press it again, or **×**, to close.

[Show me](show:tweaks)
:::

:::phone
Tap **⋯** in the top bar, then **Tweaks**. Tap **×** to close.
:::

Tweaks opens from a match. On the home screen, only the theme can be changed (the **Auto / Day / Night** switch at the top).

## What's in it

- **Theme**: **Auto**, **Day** or **Night**. See [Day, night and accent](help:theme).
- **Accent**: the colour of the main buttons and highlights. See [Day, night and accent](help:theme).
- **Card size**: how big cards are. See [Card size and Art / Text](help:card-display).
- **Card display**: **Art** or **Text** for every card. See [Card size and Art / Text](help:card-display).
- **Challenge forecast**: **Off**, **Light** or **Full**. See [Challenge forecast](help:forecast).
- **Player palette**: **Competition**, **Modern** or **Mono**. See [Player palette](help:palette).

## Good to know

- A change shows straight away; there's nothing to save.
- Tweaks are kept in this browser. They don't go to your account, and clearing the site's data resets them.
