**Challenge forecast** sets how much the Dojo tells you about a [challenge](help:challenge) before you make it: who would be banished and how much damage each card would have.

## Choose how much

In [Tweaks](help:tweaks), **Challenge forecast** has three choices: **Off**, **Light** (the default) and **Full**.

[Show me](show:tweak-forecast)

:::mouse
While you drag your character onto an opponent's card, or point at one in challenge mode:

- **Off** shows only the arrow.
- **Light** shows one line over the target: who gets banished, and the damage after.
- **Full** shows a forecast card with each card's damage before and after, marked **BANISHED** or **SURVIVES**, and the extra damage on both cards. Once you pick an attacker in challenge mode, every opponent card also gets an outcome badge.

The challenge mode bar has the same **Off / Light / Full** switch, so you can change it mid-challenge.

[Show me](show:challenge-banner)
:::

:::touch
With no pointer to hover, **Light** and **Full** both work the same way: once you pick your character in challenge mode, every opponent card gets an outcome badge. **Off** shows no badges.
:::

## Good to know

- The forecast only predicts. The Dojo never stops a challenge: if your character is exerted or drying, it lets you and says so.
- The setting is kept in this browser.
