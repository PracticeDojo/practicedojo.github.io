The **Theme** setting picks a light or dark Dojo; **Accent** picks the colour that marks the main button and where you are.

## Choose the theme

In [Tweaks](help:tweaks), **Theme** has three choices:

- **Auto** follows your device's light or dark setting, and changes when it does.
- **Day** is always the warm light look.
- **Night** is always the warm dark look.

[Show me](show:tweak-theme)

On the home screen, the **Auto / Day / Night** switch at the top is the same setting.

[Show me](show:home-theme)

## Choose the accent

**Accent** has four swatches. Its current name shows beside the label.

- **Signal** is the theme's own: orange by day, a brighter orange at night.
- **Coral**, **Olive** and **Trace** use that colour in both themes.

The accent colours the main button in each view, like **End turn** and **Start match**, and the marks that show where you are, like the turn number and the line you're on in the Multiverse.

[Show me](show:tweak-accent)

## Good to know

- Your choices are kept in this browser. The Practice Dojo website follows the same theme.
- The players' colours are a separate setting: [Player palette](help:palette).
