There's no computer opponent in the Dojo. You play Player 1's turn, end it, then play Player 2's turn, and so on. That's how you test a line against the best answer you can think of.

## Whose turn it is

The turn chip in the top bar says it: `TURN 3 → Player 2`. The player to play always sits on the **bottom** half of the board, labelled `TO PLAY`; the other player sits at the top, labelled `WAITING`.

[Show me](show:turn)

When you end the turn, the halves swap: the other player moves to the bottom and their turn begins. See [End your turn](help:end-turn).

## The other player's hand

The waiting player's hand is face down at the top of the board.

:::desktop
Hold **Hold to Reveal Hand** to see it, and let go to hide it again.
:::

:::phone
Tap **Hold to Reveal Hand** to show it, and tap again to hide it.
:::

Peeking is noted in the log. The hand hides again when the turn ends.

## Good to know

- You can still act on the waiting player's cards: damage them, banish them, change their lore or draw for them. The Dojo doesn't stop you.
- The sidebar lists **Player 1 (You)** and **Player 2**. "You" is just the seat that goes first.
