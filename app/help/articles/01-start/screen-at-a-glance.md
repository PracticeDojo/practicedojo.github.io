The board is split in two. The player whose turn it is sits at the bottom, and the other player sits at the top. When the turn ends, the two halves swap.

## The top bar

:::desktop
- **Left.** **‹ Dojo** goes back to the home screen. Next come the session's title, which you click to rename, then **Save**, **Save as copy** and **Share**.
- **Centre.** **Undo**, the turn chip and **Challenge**. The turn chip reads like `TURN 16 → Player 2`.
- **Right.** **Multiverse** and **Tweaks**.
:::

:::phone
From left to right, the bar holds **☰** for the side panel, **Undo**, the turn chip, **Challenge**, **Multiverse** and **⋯**. The **⋯** menu holds **Save**, **Save as copy**, **Share link**, **Tweaks** and **Leave to the dojo**.
:::

## Each player's half

:::desktop
Each half is labelled `P1 · TO PLAY` or `P2 · WAITING`. It holds these parts:

- **The play area**, where characters, items and locations sit.
- **Discard**, a list of the cards in it, with a magnifier to look through them.
- **The ink bar**, with the ready ink count, **−** and **+**, **Ready all** and the ink cards. The count reads like `4 /7 READY`.
- **Deck**, with its count. Click it to draw.
- **Lore**, the big number, with **−** and **+**.

The bottom half also has your hand and the **Turn N notes** box. The top half has the other player's hand face down, with **Hold to Reveal Hand**.
:::

:::phone
Each player has one bar. It holds **Lore** with **−** and **+**, then **Ink** with its count and pips, then the discard with its top card and count, then the deck. Tap **Ink** for the inkwell. Tap the deck to draw, or **⋮** for more. Under the board, the hand tray holds **Quest**, `Hand · N` and **End turn**, and your hand peeks up from the bottom edge.
:::

## The side panels

:::desktop
The left rail holds the players with their deck and hand counts, the mini multiverse, the lore race, the card preview, the game log, **Auto-save every turn**, **Quest** and **End turn**. Hover a card to see its details in the card preview. The right pane shows **Draw Odds** for the player to play.
:::

:::phone
**☰** opens the side panel. It holds the players, the card preview, the game log, **Auto-save every turn**, **Quest** and **End turn**. Phones have no draw odds pane, mini multiverse, lore race or notes box. To write a note on a phone, see [Turn notes](help:turn-notes).
:::

## Good to know

- The colour of a half follows the player, not the position. Player 1 keeps their colour when they move to the top.
- On a phone or tablet, tap a card to open the card drawer with all its actions. See [Act on a card in play](help:card-actions).
