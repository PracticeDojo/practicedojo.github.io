The board is split in two: the player whose turn it is sits at the bottom, the other player at the top. When the turn ends, the two halves swap.

## The top bar

:::desktop
- **Left:** **‹ Dojo** back to the home screen, the session's title (click to rename), then **Save**, **Save as copy** and **Share**.
- **Centre:** **Undo**, the turn chip (`TURN 16 → Player 2`) and **Challenge**.
- **Right:** **Multiverse** and **Tweaks**.
:::

:::phone
From left to right: **☰** (the side panel), **Undo**, the turn chip, **Challenge**, **Multiverse** and **⋯**. The **⋯** menu holds **Save**, **Save as copy**, **Share link**, **Tweaks** and **Leave to the dojo**.
:::

## Each player's half

:::desktop
Each half is labelled `P1 · TO PLAY` or `P2 · WAITING`. It holds:

- **The play area**, where characters, items and locations sit.
- **Discard**: a list of the cards in it, with a magnifier to look through them.
- **The ink bar**: ready ink (`4 /7 READY`), **−** / **+**, **Ready all** and the ink cards.
- **Deck**: the count. Click it to draw.
- **Lore**: the big number, with **−** and **+**.

The bottom half also has your hand and the **Turn N notes** box. The top half has the other player's hand face down, with **Hold to Reveal Hand**.
:::

:::phone
Each player has one bar: **Lore** with **−** / **+**, **Ink** with its count and pips (tap it for the inkwell), then the discard (its top card and count) and the deck (tap to draw, **⋮** for more). Under the board, the hand tray holds **Quest**, `Hand · N` and **End turn**, with your hand peeking up from the bottom edge.
:::

## The side panels

:::desktop
The left rail holds the players (deck and hand counts), the mini multiverse, the card preview (hover a card for details), the game log, **Auto-save every turn**, **Quest** and **End turn**. The right pane shows **Draw Odds** for the player to play.
:::

:::phone
**☰** opens the side panel: the players, the card preview, the game log, **Auto-save every turn**, **Quest** and **End turn**. Phones have no draw odds pane, mini multiverse or notes box; see [Turn notes](help:turn-notes) for writing a note.
:::

## Good to know

- The colour of a half follows the player, not the position: Player 1 keeps their colour when they move to the top.
- On a phone or tablet, tap a card to open the card drawer with all its actions. See [Act on a card in play](help:card-actions).
