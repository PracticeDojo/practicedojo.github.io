The Practice Dojo is a Lorcana sandbox. You put two decks on the table and play the game out by hand, both sides, at your own pace.

## What you can do

- **Practise a match.** Seat your deck and an opponent's, press **Start match**, and play both players' turns. See [Start your first match](help:first-match).
- **Rewind and try another line.** Every turn you end is saved to the **Multiverse**. Go back to any turn, press **Play from here**, and play it differently. The old line stays. See [What the Multiverse is](help:multiverse).
- **Study a Duels.ink game.** Paste a Duels.ink log or drop a replay file on the home screen, and every turn of that game becomes a turn you can open and replay. See [Study a Duels.ink game](help:import-overview).
- **Practise an opener.** Mulligan with real draw odds, or pick your seven by hand. See [Mulligan and your opening hand](help:mulligan).

## You are the referee

The Dojo keeps the board, counts ink, damage and lore, and saves every turn. It doesn't play for the other side and it doesn't stop you breaking a rule: you can play a card you can't afford, ink twice in a turn, or challenge with a character that's still drying. When a challenge breaks a rule, the log says so.

## Good to know

- Nothing to install and no account needed. Your sessions and decks are kept in this browser. Signing in is optional.
- Card text and art come from an online card database, so the first visit needs a connection.
