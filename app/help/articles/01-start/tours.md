A guided tour points at the real controls one at a time and waits while you try each one. There are four tours, and each takes a few minutes:

- **Your first turn** covers the mulligan, the board, inking and playing a card, reading a card, ending the turn, playing the other side, [Undo](help:undo) and where to find the [Multiverse](help:multiverse).
- **You're the referee** opens a game in progress where you carry out the cards' effects yourself. You draw for **The Horseman Strikes!** and pick the character with Evasive. The Dojo lets you pick the wrong one. Then you put actions in the discard, quest, exert, return a character to your hand, shift, challenge, and set lore by hand.
- **The Multiverse** runs on a copy of the Set 14 demo. You open the Multiverse, read and select a node, press **Play from here**, and see branches, saving the turn you're in and the plot view. It ends with the mini map on desktop or the thumb keys on a phone.
- **Study a Duels.ink game** covers the paste field, the example log, the readout, **Study this game** and the imported turns in the Multiverse.

## Start a tour

:::desktop
Open Help with the **?** in the top bar, or press {key:?}. The tours are at the top of the list. A ✓ marks the ones you've finished.
:::

:::phone
Open Help from the **⋯** menu, or press **Help** on the home screen. The tours are at the top of the list. A ✓ marks the ones you've finished.
:::

The first time you open the Dojo, a welcome offers the first tour. Press **Start the tour**, or press **Explore on my own** and it won't ask again. Your first **Start match** asks once more, unless you've done *Your first turn*. **Take the tour first** starts your match, runs the tour, then brings you back to the match. **Just play** starts the match without the tour. Until you first open Help, its button pulses orange.

## During a tour

- Each step names the control to use. Steps that wait for you have **Skip this step**. The other steps have **Next** and **Back**.
- **Learn more** opens the article for that step. Close Help and the tour carries on.

:::desktop
Leave any time with the **✕** on the tour card, or press {key:Esc}.
:::

:::phone
Leave any time with the **✕** on the tour card.
:::

## Good to know

- A tour plays on a practice board, marked **Practice · not saved**. Nothing you do there goes in your library. The match you had open waits, unchanged, until the tour ends.
- To keep the practice board, press **Save a copy** in its banner. It makes the board a session of your own and ends the tour.
- Reloading the page ends a tour. Start it again from Help.
