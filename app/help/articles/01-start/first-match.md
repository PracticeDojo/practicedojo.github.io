A match starts on the home screen: put a deck in each seat under **On the table**, then press **Start match**.

## Seat two decks

**On the table** has two seats: **You** (Player 1) and **Opponent** (Player 2).

- **Pick a built-in or saved deck:** press one of the deck chips under the seats. It goes to the first open seat. With both seats taken, it replaces the opponent.
- **Paste a decklist:** paste it into the big field at the top (one card per line, like `4 Tinker Bell - Giant Fairy`). The readout under it says how many cards it recognised. Press **Play it as you** or **Play against it**, or **Save to My decks** to keep it.
- **No list to hand?** Press **A decklist** under the field to try an example.

[Show me](show:seats)

Each seat shows the deck's name and card count. The count is marked when it isn't 60. Press **×** on a seat to empty it.

## Start the match

Press **Start match**. Both players draw seven, and Player 1's turn opens on the mulligan: keep the hand or send cards back. See [Mulligan and your opening hand](help:mulligan).

[Show me](show:start-match)

## Good to know

- **You** is Player 1 and goes first. You play both sides; see [You play both sides](help:you-play-both-sides).
- An empty seat plays 60 random cards.
- Next time, the home screen seats the same saved decks again (a pasted list isn't kept unless you save it).
- The match saves itself to this device as you play. Come back to it from **Pick up again** on the home screen.
