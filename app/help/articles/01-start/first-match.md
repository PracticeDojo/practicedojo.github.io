A match starts on the home screen. Put a deck in each seat under **On the table**, then press **Start match**.

## Seat two decks

**On the table** has two seats. **You** is Player 1 and **Opponent** is Player 2.

- **Pick a built-in or saved deck.** Press one in the deck list under the seats. It goes to the first open seat. With both seats taken, it replaces the opponent. **Your decks** come first. **Default decks** fold away with their heading, and stay folded next time.
- **Find one.** Type in **Find a deck or a card in one**. It matches deck names and the cards in each deck, and it allows typos and missing letters. For example, `lvthn` finds The Leviathan. A deck found by a card names the card and how many copies the deck has. The ink buttons beside the field narrow the list. One ink shows the decks that use it, and two show that exact pair.
- **Paste a decklist.** Paste it into the big field at the top, one card per line, like `4 Tinker Bell - Giant Fairy`. A list copied from Duels.ink, with `(1-193)` after each name, works too. The readout under the field says how many cards it recognised. Press **Play it as you** or **Play against it**, or **Save to My decks** to keep it.
- **Try an example.** With no list to hand, press **A decklist** under the field.

Each seat shows the deck's name and card count. The count is marked when it isn't 60. Press **×** on a seat to empty it.

## Start the match

Press **Start match**. Both players draw seven, and Player 1's turn opens on the mulligan. Keep the hand or send cards back. See [Mulligan and your opening hand](help:mulligan).

## Good to know

- **You** is Player 1 and goes first. You play both sides. See [You play both sides](help:you-play-both-sides).
- An empty seat plays 60 random cards.
- Next time, the home screen seats the same saved decks again. A pasted list isn't kept unless you save it.
- The Dojo saves the match to this device as you play. Come back to it from **Pick up again** on the home screen.
