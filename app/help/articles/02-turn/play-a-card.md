Play a card from your hand to put it into play. If you have enough ready ink, the Dojo spends the card's cost for you.

## Play a card

:::desktop
Drag the card from your hand onto your play area; anywhere in the area takes it. Or hover the card and press **PLAY**, or click it and choose **Play Card (-3 Ink)**.
:::

:::phone
Tap the card in your hand to open the card drawer. It shows the cost and your ready ink. Press **Play · −3 ink**. Or drag the card up onto the board.
:::

[Show me](show:hand)

A character comes in **drying**: it shows a **NEW** tag and can't quest until your next turn. Songs and actions land in play too; when they're done, banish them to send them to the discard.

## Not enough ink?

You can still play it. The Dojo plays the card and spends no ink, so you can test a line you can't quite afford. On a phone the key says so: **Play anyway · 2 short**.

## Good to know

- To shift a card or play a character straight to a location, drag it onto that card instead. See [Locations, shift and cards under](help:locations-and-stacks).
- Card abilities don't trigger by themselves. Do what the card says by hand: draw, add damage, change lore.
- **Undo** takes the play back, ink included.
