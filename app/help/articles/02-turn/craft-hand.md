Crafting lets you choose your opening hand card by card, so you can practise one opener again and again.

## Craft your opening hand

1. When your mulligan opens, press **Craft hand** (**Craft** on a phone). [Show me](show:craft-hand)
2. **Craft Starting Hand** shows every different card in your deck, cheapest first. Each says how many you've picked of how many the deck has (`1/4`).
3. Add a copy by clicking (or tapping) the card. You can't pick more copies than the deck has, or more than seven cards.

:::mouse
Right-click a card to take one copy back out.
:::

:::touch
To start over, press **Clear**.
:::

4. When **Selected** reads `7 / 7`, press **Confirm Hand**. The rest of the deck is shuffled and your turn starts. Player 2 draws for the turn first, as usual.

## Copy an opening from a saved game

Replay the exact luck of another game: the same seven in hand and the same deck order underneath, so you see the same draws. Use it to test whether another line, or another deck, beats the draws a deck had.

1. In **Craft Starting Hand**, press **From a saved game**.
2. **Pick a game**: your saved games on this device, then the demos. The game on the board and Duels.ink imports aren't listed (an import doesn't know the hidden deck order).
3. **Pick a deck**: either player's deck from that game. It says whether that deck went first or was on the draw, and how many openings it has.
4. **Pick an opening**: each different opening that deck had in the game's Multiverse, labelled **kept**, **mull 2** and so on, with its seven cards. When there's only one, this step is skipped.

The hand and deck order land on the seat that's on its mulligan. Going second, you still draw your top card for the turn; going first, you don't.

- If the opening is from a different deck, the Dojo asks whether to **Switch deck**. Yes puts that exact deck on your seat (your opponent's deck stays). **Cancel** changes nothing.
- Only the opening is copied. Shuffles later in the game are random again.
- An older game whose deck was shuffled or reordered on turn 1 can't give back its order; that opening shows greyed out.
- The log and the turn's recap say where the opening came from. **Back** steps back through the picker.

## Good to know

- **Back** (or **×**) returns to the mulligan without changing anything.
- Crafting takes the place of the mulligan: once you confirm, the opening hand is settled.
- Changed your mind? **Undo** brings the mulligan back.
