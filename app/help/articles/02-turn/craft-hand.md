The opening-hand dialog makes your hand in three ways. The switch at its top picks one. **Draw** deals seven and offers the mulligan. **Build** lets you pick the seven yourself. **Replay** copies an opening from another game. The dialog always opens on **Draw**.

## Build your seven

Pick an opener card by card to practise it again and again.

1. When your mulligan opens, press **Build**. [Show me](show:craft-hand)
2. Your hand is in the middle, as seven empty slots. Your deck is on the right, or below the hand on a phone, cheapest first. The dots under a card count its copies. A filled dot is a copy in your hand.
3. Click or tap a card in the deck to put it in the next slot. Click a card in your hand to take it out.
4. **Find a card** searches by name. On desktop, the cost buttons show one cost at a time.
5. **Fill the rest at random** fills the empty slots from the rest of the deck. A phone labels it **Random**. Pick the two cards you care about and let the rest be random.
6. When all seven are in, press **Keep this hand**. The Dojo shuffles the rest of the deck and your turn starts. Player 2 draws for the turn first, as usual.

## Replay an opening

Replay copies another game's opening. You get the same seven in hand and the same deck order underneath, so you see the same draws. Use it to test whether another line, or another deck, beats the draws a deck had.

1. When your mulligan opens, press **Replay**. [Show me](show:replay-opening)
2. The list shows every opening in your saved games on this device, then in the demos, grouped by game. Each one names its deck, says whether it was on the play or the draw, and says how the hand was settled, such as **kept**, **mull 2** or **built**. The list leaves out the game on the board and Duels.ink imports. An import doesn't know the hidden deck order.
3. **Your deck** shows only openings of the deck you have here. **All** shows every deck. **Find a game or deck** searches the list.
4. Pick an opening. Its seven cards show in the middle with their curve. **Your next draws** shows the next four cards, labelled by the turn you'd draw them on. On a phone the opening opens by itself, and **Openings** goes back to the list.
5. Press **Play this opening**. If you go second, you still draw your top card for the turn. If you go first, you don't.

If the opening is from a different deck, the button says **Switch deck and play** and the line beside it names the deck you get. Your opponent's deck stays as it is.

## Good to know

- Replay copies only the opening. Shuffles later in the game are random again.
- An older game whose deck was shuffled or reordered on turn 1 can't give back its order. That opening shows greyed out.
- The log and the turn's recap say where a replayed opening came from.
- Building or replaying takes the place of the mulligan. Once you keep the hand, it's settled. **Undo** brings the mulligan back.
