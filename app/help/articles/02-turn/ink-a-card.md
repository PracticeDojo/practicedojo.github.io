Ink a card to grow your inkwell. Ink pays for the cards you play. A card goes in ready, so you can spend it the turn you ink it.

## Ink a card

:::desktop
Drag a card from your hand onto your ink bar; anywhere on the bar takes it. Or hover the card and press **INK**, or click it and choose **Add to Inkwell**.
:::

:::phone
Tap the card in your hand. In the card drawer, press **Ink**. Or drag the card up onto the ink count on your player bar.
:::

[Show me](show:ink-bar)

## See what you inked this turn

A card inked this turn shows face up in the inkwell, and the count gets a tag: **+1** on desktop, **+1 inked** on a phone. Until you ink, a phone's bar says **No ink yet**. The cards turn face down when that player's next turn begins.

## Good to know

- The Dojo doesn't enforce one ink a turn: the tag just shows how many you've inked.
- **INK**, **Add to Inkwell** and **Ink** only appear for a card with the inkwell symbol (a phone shows **No ink** instead). Dragging doesn't check, so you can ink any card.
- To take it back, press **Undo**, or move the ink card to your hand. See [Your inkwell](help:inkwell).
