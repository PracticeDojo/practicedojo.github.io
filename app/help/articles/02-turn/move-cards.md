Card effects move cards between zones all the time. Any card in play or in a hand can go back to the hand, onto the top or bottom of the deck, or into the discard.

## From play

:::desktop
Right-click the card. Under **Move**: **Banish (To Discard)**, **Return to Hand**, **Put on Top of Deck**, **Put on Bottom of Deck**.
:::

:::touch
Tap the card. In the card drawer, **Banish**, or under **Move to**: **To hand**, **Deck top**, **Deck bottom**.
:::

## From a hand

:::desktop
Right-click the card in your hand: **Discard Card**, **Put on Top of Deck** or **Put on Bottom of Deck**.
:::

:::phone
Tap the card in your hand. In the card drawer, **Discard**, or under **More**: **Deck top** or **Deck bottom**.
:::

## By dragging

:::desktop
Drag a card onto a deck (it goes on top), a discard pile or a hand. This works from play, a hand or the inkwell.
:::

## Good to know

- A card leaving play loses its damage and exert, and takes any cards under it along.
- The menu and the drawer send a card to its own player's deck, hand or discard. A drag puts it where you drop it.
- Ink cards go to hand or discard from the inkwell. See [Your inkwell](help:inkwell).
- To bring a card back from the discard, see [Your discard pile](help:inspect-discard).
- **Undo** takes any move back.
