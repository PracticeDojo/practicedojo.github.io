When you're done, end the turn. The other player's turn begins, and the board swaps so they're at the bottom.

## End the turn

:::desktop
Press **End turn →** at the foot of the left rail, or press {key:Space}.
:::

:::phone
Press **End turn** in the hand tray, right of your hand. It's also in the **☰** panel.
:::

[Show me](show:end-turn)

## What happens next

1. The other player becomes the player to play. The turn number goes up when Player 1's turn comes round.
2. **Ready step:** their characters and items ready, drying ends, and their ink readies. Cards they inked last turn turn face down. Locations aren't readied.
3. **Draw step:** they draw a card.
4. **Auto-save:** the Multiverse gets a node for the new turn, named like `Turn 4 - Player 1 Active`. It keeps your turn note and a recap of what you did (drew, inked, played, quested, banished). See [Every turn is saved](help:auto-save).

Challenge mode ends, and the other player's hand is hidden again.

## Auto-save every turn

The switch **Auto-save every turn** sits in the left rail (in the **☰** panel on a phone). It's on by default. Turn it off and ending the turn saves no node.

[Show me](show:auto-save)

## Good to know

- Player 2's first turn opens on their mulligan; their draw comes after it. See [Mulligan](help:mulligan).
- The turn can't end while a mulligan is open.
- Abilities that trigger at the start or end of a turn aren't applied: do them by hand.
- Ended too soon? **Undo** takes you back. The node it saved stays in the Multiverse.
