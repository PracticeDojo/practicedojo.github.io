The first player to reach 20 lore wins. The Dojo notices, whether the lore came from a quest, **Quest** or the lore **+**.

## When a player reaches 20

A dialog says who won, the lore score and the turn. A **Game Over - Player 1 Wins!** node is saved to the Multiverse, whether auto-save is on or not. Then choose:

- **View Multiverse** to see the game as a tree and pick another turn to replay.
- **Stay Here** to keep playing on this board.

[Show me](show:lore)

## Try another line

A win is the end of one branch, not of the session. Open the **Multiverse**, select an earlier turn and press **Play from here** to play it differently. See [What the Multiverse is](help:multiverse).

## Good to know

- The Dojo doesn't stop the game at 20: the board stays live.
- It says so once per branch. If you **Undo** below 20 and reach it again, you get the dialog and a new node again.
- Lore isn't capped at 20, and other ways to win or lose (like an empty deck) aren't checked.
