The first player to reach 20 lore wins. The Dojo notices whether the lore came from a quest, **Quest** or the lore **+**.

## When a player reaches 20

A dialog names the winner, the lore score and the turn. The Dojo saves a **Game Over - Player 1 Wins!** node to the Multiverse, whether auto-save is on or not. Then choose one of these:

- **View Multiverse** shows the game as a tree, where you pick another turn to replay.
- **Stay Here** keeps you playing on this board.

[Show me](show:lore)

## Try another line

A win ends one branch, and the session goes on. Open the **Multiverse**, select an earlier turn and press **Play from here** to play it differently. See [What the Multiverse is](help:multiverse).

## Good to know

- The Dojo doesn't stop the game at 20. The board stays live.
- The dialog shows once per branch. If you **Undo** below 20 and reach it again, you get the dialog and a new node again.
- Lore isn't capped at 20. The Dojo doesn't check other ways to win or lose, such as an empty deck.
