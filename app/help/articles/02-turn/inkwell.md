Your inkwell shows how much ink you have ready out of your total. Playing a card spends ink for you. Use these controls for everything else, such as an ability that costs ink.

## Read your ink

:::desktop
The ink bar reads like `4 /7 READY`, which means four ready out of seven. Spent ink cards are dimmed and turned. The ink cards sit on the right of the bar, and overlap once they don't fit.
:::

:::phone
Your player bar shows a count like `4/7` and a pip for each ink. A spent ink's pip is dimmed.
:::

[Show me](show:ink-bar)

## Spend or ready ink

:::desktop
On the ink bar, **−** spends one ink, **+** readies one, and **Ready all** readies all of them.
:::

:::phone
Tap the ink count to open the inkwell. **−** spends one, **+** readies one, and **Ready all** readies all of them. **×** or a tap outside closes it.
:::

## Act on one ink card

:::desktop
Click an ink card. It opens in place with **Exert** or **Ready**, **To hand** and **Discard**. Click it again, click elsewhere or press {key:Esc} to put it down.
:::

:::phone
In the inkwell, tap an ink card. Then press **Exert** or **Ready**, **To hand** or **Discard** in the bar underneath.
:::

## Ink drops

When a card in either deck mentions ink drops, each player gets an ink drops counter, kept apart from their ink. Hyperia City is one such card. Without such a card, the counter doesn't show.

:::desktop
The **Ink drops** box sits in the ink bar, left of the deck. **−** and **+** change the count.
:::

:::phone
The teardrop count sits in your player bar, between your ink and your piles. **−** and **+** change it. The ink pips hide to make room, and the `ready/total` count stays.
:::

## Good to know

- Both players' inkwells work the same way, so you can fix the waiting player's ink too.
- Ink readies by itself at the start of that player's turn.
- If you play a card you can't afford, the Dojo spends no ink. Spend it by hand with **−** if you need to.
