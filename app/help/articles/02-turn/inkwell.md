Your inkwell shows how much ink you have ready out of how much you have. Playing a card spends ink for you; these controls are for everything else, like an ability that costs ink.

## Read your ink

:::desktop
The ink bar reads `4 /7 READY`: four ready of seven. Spent ink cards are dimmed and turned. The ink cards sit on the right of the bar, overlapping once they don't fit.
:::

:::phone
Your player bar shows `4/7` and a pip for each ink, dimmed when spent.
:::

[Show me](show:ink-bar)

## Spend or ready ink

:::desktop
On the ink bar, **−** spends one ink, **+** readies one, and **Ready all** readies the lot.
:::

:::phone
Tap the ink count to open the inkwell. **−** spends one, **+** readies one, **Ready all** readies the lot. **×** or a tap outside closes it.
:::

## Act on one ink card

:::desktop
Click an ink card. It opens in place with **Exert** (or **Ready**), **To hand** and **Discard**. Click it again, click elsewhere or press {key:Esc} to put it down.
:::

:::phone
In the inkwell, tap an ink card, then **Exert** (or **Ready**), **To hand** or **Discard** in the bar underneath.
:::

## Good to know

- Both players' inkwells work the same way, so you can fix the waiting player's ink too.
- Ink readies by itself at the start of that player's turn.
- If you play a card you can't afford, no ink is spent. Spend it by hand with **−** if you need to.
