Sign in to keep your decks and sessions in an account. It's optional; see [Why sign in](help:account).

## Sign in

1. On the home screen, press **Sign in** at the top. (The Library's **Your account** shelf has a **Sign in** button too.)
2. Choose **Continue with Discord** or **Continue with Google**.
3. Approve the Dojo on Discord's or Google's page. You come back to the Dojo, signed in, and the button shows your name.

[Show me](show:account-button)

The Dojo gets your name, picture and email from Discord or Google. It never sees your password.

## Bring what's on this device

The first time you sign in on a device that already has decks or sessions, the Dojo asks whether to save them to your account:

- **All** saves every one.
- **Choose…** opens the Library, where **Save to account** (in a row's **⋯**) or **Save all N to account** (on the **Only on this device** shelf) saves one row or a whole shelf.
- **Not now** leaves them on this device only. You can still save them from the Library later.

Either way, they stay on this device too.

## Your account

Press your name on the home screen to see who you're signed in as and how much of your account you use (up to 50 sessions and 200 decks).

## Sign out

Press your name, then **Sign out**. The Dojo asks about the copies of your account's decks and sessions on this device:

- **Keep on this device**: they stay here, for you or anyone using this browser.
- **Remove from this device**: use this on a shared computer. Your account keeps everything either way.

Sessions and decks that were never in an account stay on the device either way.

## Good to know

- Share links you made from this browser before signing in move to your account. If your Discord or Google account was already a Dojo account, the Dojo offers to sign in to it instead, and those links stay with this browser.
