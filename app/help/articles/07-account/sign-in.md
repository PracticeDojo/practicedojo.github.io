Sign in to keep your decks and sessions in an account. An account is optional. See [Why sign in](help:account).

## Sign in

1. On the home screen, press **Sign in** at the top. The Library's **Your account** shelf also has a **Sign in** button.
2. Choose **Continue with Discord** or **Continue with Google**.
3. Approve the Dojo on Discord's or Google's page. You come back to the Dojo, signed in, and the button shows your name.

[Show me](show:account-button)

The Dojo gets your name, picture and email from Discord or Google. It never sees your password.

## Bring what's on this device

The first time you sign in on a device that already has decks or sessions, the Dojo asks whether to save them to your account:

- **All** saves every one.
- **Choose…** opens the Library. **Save to account** in a row's **⋯** saves one row. **Save all N to account** on the **Only on this device** shelf saves the whole shelf.
- **Not now** leaves them on this device only. You can still save them from the Library later.

Whichever you choose, they also stay on this device.

## Your account

Press your name on the home screen to see who you're signed in as and how much of your account you use. An account holds up to 50 sessions and 200 decks.

## Sign out

Press your name, then **Sign out**. The Dojo asks what to do with the copies of your account's decks and sessions on this device:

- **Keep on this device** leaves them here, for you or anyone using this browser.
- **Remove from this device** is for a shared computer. The Dojo first saves to your account any changes it doesn't have yet. Anything it can't save stays on this device, and the Dojo tells you how many.

Sessions and decks that were never in an account stay on the device in both cases.

## Good to know

- Share links you made from this browser before signing in move to your account. If your Discord or Google account was already a Dojo account, the Dojo offers to sign in to it instead, and those links stay with this browser.
