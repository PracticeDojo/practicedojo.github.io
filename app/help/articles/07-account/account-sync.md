When you're signed in, decks reach your account by themselves, and sessions reach it when you press **Save**. Nothing merges in the background, so you always know which copy is where.

## Decks follow you

Saving a deck on this device also saves it to your account. Your other devices pick it up when you sign in or open the Library's **Decks** tab. If two devices edit the same deck, the last save wins. Deleting a deck that's in your account deletes it from both places.

## Sessions go up when you press Save

While you play, the Dojo saves the session to this device only. **Save** in the top bar also saves it to your account. On a phone, open **⋯** and choose **Save**. If a session is already in your account and has unsaved changes, the Dojo saves it there again when you switch tabs or close the page.

## Read the Library

In **Sessions**, a row says when its two copies differ:

- **Changed here since your last Save** means this device has changes. Press **Save to account**.
- **Your account has a newer copy** means another device saved later. Press **Get newer copy** to replace this device's copy with it.
- On the **Only in your account** shelf, **Download & open** brings a session to this device.

## When two devices disagree

If the session changed in your account since this device last saved it, **Save** asks **This session changed on another device**. Choose one:

- **Overwrite with mine** makes your account keep this device's version.
- **Save mine as a copy** sends this version to your account as a new session, and the other version stays.
- **Cancel** saves nothing to your account.

## Good to know

- Deleting a session that's in both places asks **This device**, **Account** or **Both**.
- If saving to your account fails, the match is still saved on this device.
- An account holds up to 50 sessions and 200 decks, and a session up to 10 MB compressed.
