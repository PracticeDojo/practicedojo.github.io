An account is optional. It keeps your decks and sessions somewhere other than this browser, so they're on your other devices too: every device you sign in on.

## Signed out, everything works

You can play, use the Multiverse, study Duels.ink games, keep decks and sessions in the Library, export files, and make and open share links, all without an account. Signed out, nothing about accounts loads: the Dojo only goes online to load itself and the cards, and when you make or open a share link.

The catch: your library lives only in this browser on this device. Clearing the site's data, or closing a private window, deletes it, and another browser or device doesn't have it. Exporting files is the way to keep a backup. See [Where your things are kept](help:storage).

## What signing in adds

- **Decks follow you.** A deck you save goes to your account by itself and shows up on your other devices.
- **Sessions when you press Save.** **Save** puts the match in your account; play on another device and open it from the Library.
- **The Library shows where things are.** Its shelves sort everything by place: in your account, only on this device, or only in your account.
- **Your share links** belong to your account.

The details are in [What goes to your account](help:account-sync).

## Good to know

- You sign in with Discord or Google. See [Sign in and out](help:sign-in).
- An account holds up to 50 sessions and 200 decks. Sessions that don't fit can stay on this device.
- Your Tweaks (theme, card size and the rest) stay with this browser; they don't go to your account.
- Signing in needs a connection. If the account can't be reached, everything on this device still works.
